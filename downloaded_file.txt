In the modern age of interconnected devices and ever-growing data streams, the architecture of distributed systems plays a central role in enabling scalable, resilient, and efficient applications. Designing such systems requires more than assembling services; it demands careful consideration of communication patterns, fault tolerance strategies, and data consistency models. When engineers choose an approach—whether event-driven, request-reply, or peer-to-peer—they must balance latency, throughput, and complexity.
